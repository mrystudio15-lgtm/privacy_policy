Privacy Policy for EngiNex Calc

Last updated: August 31, 2026

EngiNex Calc ("the app") is an offline engineering calculator toolbox. This policy explains, in plain terms, what happens to your data when you use it.

1. No personal data is collected

The app does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information about you. It does not require an account, a login, or any personal details to use.

2. No internet access except for purchases

The app declares exactly one network permission (INTERNET), used solely to communicate with Google Play Billing when you choose to purchase a Pro subscription or lifetime unlock. Every calculator, reference table, and the bundled materials database work entirely offline and never make a network request.

3. Purchases are handled by Google Play

If you buy a subscription or lifetime unlock, that transaction is processed entirely by Google Play Billing. The app itself never sees or stores your payment details (card numbers, billing address, etc.) — only Google does, under Google's own Privacy Policy.

4. Data stored on your device

The app stores the following locally, on your device only, and never transmits it anywhere:

  • Saved calculations ("snapshots") you choose to save
  • Recently-used tools and calculation history
  • Which tools are unlocked for your account (subscription status)
  • App preferences (unit system, theme, etc.)

This data is deleted automatically if you uninstall the app, and is never backed up to any server we control.

5. No analytics, no advertising, no tracking

The app contains no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, and no third-party tracking of any kind. We have no way to know how you use the app, and we don't try to.

6. Children's privacy

The app is a general-purpose engineering reference tool, not directed at children, and does not knowingly collect data from anyone, regardless of age — because it does not collect data from anyone at all.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes (for example, if a crash-reporting service is added in the future), the "Last updated" date above will change, and the new terms will be reflected here and in the in-app copy of this policy.
