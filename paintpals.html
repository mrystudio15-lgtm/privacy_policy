Privacy Policy

Paint Pals

Effective September 3, 2026

Paint Pals is a coloring and painting-practice app made for children. This policy explains, in plain language, what happens to information when your child uses it. The short version: almost nothing leaves the device, and we've built it that way on purpose.

No account or sign-up required
No ads, no ad tracking
No analytics or behavioral profiling
Progress is stored only on the device
1

Information we collect

Paint Pals does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on our servers — because we don't operate any servers the app talks to, and the app makes no network connections of any kind during normal use. There is no account system, no sign-in, and no way for us to identify who is using the app.

The only data the app keeps is stored locally on your device:

  • Which figures have been colored, your child's scores, and star ratings
  • App preferences, such as the last brush or color used

This information stays in the app's private storage on the device. We never see it, and it is not synced to any cloud service or shared with us in any form.

2

Photos and sharing

When your child taps Save, their finished painting is written directly to the device's own photo gallery using Android's standard photo storage system. It never passes through us or leaves the device.

The Share button hands the finished painting to your device's normal share menu, where you choose the destination app (Messages, email, etc.) — Paint Pals itself never transmits or collects that image. Because this action leaves the app, it sits behind a simple built-in parental gate (a quick math question) so a young child can't trigger it accidentally.

3

In-app purchases

Paint Pals is free to play, with a generous free sample of figures across every level and category. A single one-time purchase unlocks the full catalog — every remaining figure, in every level and category, all at once. There is no subscription, and nothing renews or charges automatically.

Purchases are processed entirely by Google Play Billing — Paint Pals never sees or stores your payment details, card number, or billing address. Google's own privacy policy governs that transaction. A parental gate (a quick math question) sits in front of the purchase button so a young child can't start a purchase alone.

All we store locally is whether that one-time unlock has been purchased on this device, so it persists between app launches. "Restore Purchase" in Settings recovers it on a new device using your Google Play account — Paint Pals still never sees any payment information as part of that.

4

Children's privacy

Paint Pals is directed at children, and we take that seriously. In line with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play's Families Policy:

  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13
  • There are no user accounts, chat features, or ways for a child to communicate with other people through the app
  • There is no user-generated content shared publicly — artwork stays on the child's own device unless a parent actively saves or shares it
  • We do not show behaviorally-targeted advertising, because we do not show advertising at all
For parents: because nothing is collected off the device, there is no remote account or profile to request, export, or delete. Clearing the app's storage (or uninstalling it) in Android's Settings removes all local data immediately.
5

Third-party services

Paint Pals is distributed through Google Play and, if you make a purchase, uses Google Play Billing to process it. Beyond the standard services Google Play itself requires to install and run any app, Paint Pals does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking software development kits (SDKs). The app's fonts and other visual assets are bundled directly inside the app itself rather than loaded from anywhere online.

6

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes — for example, if a future version of Paint Pals adds a new feature that changes what data is handled — we'll update the effective date above and describe the change here before that version is released.
